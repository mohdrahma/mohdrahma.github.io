Privacy Policy — Render Stack

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Render Stack is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels listed on the home page to upload videos and update video metadata (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists) on those channels through the YouTube Data API.

Data accessed

YouTube channel and video information of the owner's own channels, via the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl.

Data use

Used only to manage the owner's own channels. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data storage and deletion

OAuth tokens are stored privately on the owner's own servers and are never published. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

Third-party terms

Use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
